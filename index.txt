10:T97a,<h1>Liang-Yuan “Leo” Wu 吳兩原</h1>
<p>I am now working with Prof. <a href="https://hua-shen.org/index.html">Hua Shen</a> at New York University Shanghai and with Prof. <a href="https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~profdj/">Dhruv “DJ” Jain</a> and the <a href="https://accessibility.eecs.umich.edu">Soundability Lab</a> at University of Michigan. I hold a Master's Degree in Computer Science &#x26; Engineering from the University of Michigan, and a Bachelor's Degree in Electrical Engineering from National Taiwan University.</p>
<p>My research lies at the intersection of Speech and Language Processing and Human-Computer Interaction, with a focus on building human-centered AI systems for sound and speech understanding. I work closely with Deaf and Hard of Hearing (DHH) communities to explore how people perceive, trust, and interact with audio AI systems.</p>
<p>My recent focus includes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Verbal sounds (Speech Understanding)</strong>: Developing adaptive captioning and ASR systems to improve accessibility for real-world communication. [<a href="projects/cartgpt">CARTGPT</a>, <a href="https://preprints.jmir.org/preprint/79073">MedCaption</a>, <a href="projects/evolve-captions">EvolveCaptions</a>]</li>
<li><strong>Non-verbal sound (Audio Scene Understanding)</strong>: Leveraging multimodal models to interpret complex environmental and non-verbal sounds, often with human-in-the-loop consideration to make auditory environments understandable and actionable for users. [<a href="projects/sound-narratives">SoundNarratives</a>, <a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3706598.3714268">SoundWeaver</a>, <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19971">CapTune</a>]</li>
<li><strong>Emotional cues in speech and sound</strong>: Investigating how emotional content in verbal and non-verbal audio is perceived and processed by the recent advanced multimodal models.</li>
</ul>
<p>I bring hands-on experience in deep learning for speech and audio, full-stack system development, and human-centered evaluation. My work bridges algorithmic advances with mixed-methods user studies to ensure AI systems are not only technically strong but also practically meaningful.</p>
<p><strong>I am applying for 2026 Fall CS PhD programs, focusing on sound and human-centered AI for social good.</strong> I am always excited to discuss my research and potential collaboration, please feel free to reach out!</p>
