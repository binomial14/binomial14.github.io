11:T705,<h1>Liang-Yuan “Leo” Wu <span class="chinese-name">吳兩原</span></h1>
<p>I am an incoming CS PhD student at New York University's <a href="https://steinhardt.nyu.edu/marl">Music and Audio Research Lab (MARL)</a> working with <a href="https://magdalenafuentes.com">Prof. Magdalena Fuentes</a>. I also work closely with Prof. <a href="https://hua-shen.org/index.html">Hua Shen</a> at New York University Shanghai. I study how to make AI a better <em>listener</em>—one that comprehends the nuances behind sounds, including speech, environmental noises, and tones, and interprets them in ways that are meaningful to humans.</p>
<p>My research draws on methodologies from machine learning, speech and language processing, and human-computer interaction. To achieve my goals, I train and evaluate AI models, build systems, and collaborate directly with stakeholders, such as deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals.</p>
<p>I hold an MS in CSE from University of Michigan, and a BS in EE from National Taiwan University. Originally from the beautiful island of Taiwan, I lived in the dramatic highlands of Edinburgh, the college town of Ann Arbor, and among the magnificent skyscrapers of Chicago before moving to New York. I used to play basketball competitively, but have focused on bouldering over the last two years.</p>
<p>I always welcome meaningful conversations about research, potential collaborations, or shared interests. Feel free to drop me a message on <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/binomial14/">LinkedIn</a> or <a href="mailto:leo.wu@nyu.edu">send me an email</a>! I am especially happy to chat with prospective CS PhD applicants and would love to offer support in any way I can—from <a href="/blog/phd_application">the perspective of someone who is terrible at it</a>.</p>
