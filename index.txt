10:T9b7,<p>My name is Liang-Yuan “Leo” Wu 吳兩原. I am now working with Prof. <a href="https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~profdj/">Dhruv “DJ” Jain</a> and the <a href="https://accessibility.eecs.umich.edu">Soundability Lab</a> at the AI Laboratory, University of Michigan CSE. I hold a Master's Degree in Computer Science &#x26; Engineering from the University of Michigan, and a Bachelor's Degree in Electrical Engineering from National Taiwan University.</p>
<p>I am a researcher at the intersection of Speech and Language Processing and Human-Computer Interaction, focused on building human-centered AI systems that enable more accessible and robust understanding of sound and speech. My work often involves close collaboration with the Deaf and Hard of Hearing (DHH) community to understand how people perceive, trust, and engage with audio AI systems.</p>
<p>My recent focus includes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Verbal sounds (Speech Understanding)</strong>: Developing adaptive captioning and ASR systems to improve accessibility for real-world communication. [<a href="projects/cartgpt">CARTGPT</a>, <a href="https://preprints.jmir.org/preprint/79073">MedCaption</a>, <a href="projects/evolve-captions">EvolveCaptions</a>]</li>
<li><strong>Non-verbal sound (Audio Scene Understanding)</strong>: Leveraging multimodal models to interpret complex environmental and non-verbal sounds, often with human-in-the-loop consideration to make auditory environments understandable and actionable for users. [<a href="projects/sound-narratives">SoundNarratives</a>, <a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3706598.3714268">SoundWeaver</a>, <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19971">CapTune</a>]</li>
<li><strong>Emotional cues in speech and sound</strong>: Investigating how emotional content in verbal and non-verbal audio is perceived and processed by the recent advanced multimodal models.</li>
</ul>
<p>I have hands-on experience bridging algorithm development and human-centered evaluation, with a strong foundation in deep learning, speech and audio processing, and full-stack development. I also design and conduct mixed-methods studies with real users, particularly in accessibility contexts, to ensure that AI systems are both technically robust and practically useful.</p>
<p><strong>I am applying for 2026 Fall CS PhD programs, focusing on sound and human-centered AI.</strong> I am always excited to discuss my research and potential collaboration, please feel free to reach out!</p>
