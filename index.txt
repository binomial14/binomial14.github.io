<p>I am a researcher working at the intersection of <strong>Speech, Machine Learning</strong> and <strong>Human-AI Interaction</strong>.</p>
<p>Currently, I work with Prof. <a href="https://hua-shen.org/index.html">Hua Shen</a> at New York University Shanghai and Prof. <a href="https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~profdj/">Dhruv “DJ” Jain</a> at the <a href="https://accessibility.eecs.umich.edu">Soundability Lab</a>, University of Michigan. I hold an M.S. in CSE from the University of Michigan, and a B.S. in EE from National Taiwan University.</p>
<p>My research aims to build human-centered AI systems for sound and speech understanding to bridge the gap between how people perceive audio and how machines interpret it. I am particularly interested in speech agents and multimodal models that make auditory environments more accessible, trustworthy, and interactive for all users.</p>
<p>I combine deep learning and full-stack prototyping with human-centered evaluation, ensuring that technical advances translate into meaningful real-world impact.</p>
